**A top-down tank action game inspired by World Of Tanks**

Set in an alternate Second World War, a resource-starved Poland becomes an unlikely pioneer of armored warfare. Its tanks are experimental machines built from salvage: an engine from a tractor, plates held together with tape and a lot of creativity in the field.

## Planned features

- **Tanks built from modules.** Hull, turret, engine, transmission, tracks, machine guns and optics, each with its own stats and weight. Many variants, most of them unlocked over time.
- **Real heavy armor** Randomized penetration, side armor, ricochets off sloped plates, shells that punch through cover and keep flying.
- **A world worth looking at.** 2.5D buildings with real height, soft tile blending, shadows, tread marks, fog, and later weather, day and night, and contaminated zones that need special protection.
- **Destruction.** Broken tracks, engine fires, a jammed turret ring, smashed periscopes... Fix them in the field to successfully finish your mission.
- **Scripted missions.** Ambushes, scouting convoys, explosions, burning streets, trains and air strikes that light up the sky.
- **Research.** Earn experience, stole blueprints from enemy and unlock new tank variants.
- **Building system.** Return to your base between missions to fix and improve your tanks and also fortify the base to defend it in sieges.
