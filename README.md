**Command an experimental tank built from salvage in an alternate 1940s Poland.**

Polish Tanks is a top-down tank action game where armour angles, penetration and damaged modules decide every fight. Keep your thick front towards the enemy, slip around to their weaker sides and put a shell into the engine at the back.

Set in an alternate Second World War, a resource-starved Poland becomes an unlikely pioneer of armored warfare. Its tanks are experimental machines built from salvage: an engine from a tractor, plates held together with tape and a lot of creativity in the field.

##  Features
- **Real armour:** strong front, weaker sides, a vulnerable rear with the engine. Shells bounce, stick or punch through.
- **Module damage:** engine fires, broken tracks, smashed vision blocks. Patch them in the field or reach the workshop.
- **Enemy tanks** that spot you, radio your position to each other and search for you when you break contact.
- **Missions** with primary and optional objectives, cutscenes and dynamic events
- **A sandbox** to try every existing module combination.
