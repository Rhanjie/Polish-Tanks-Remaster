Command an experimental tank built from salvage in an alternate 1940s Poland.

Polish Tanks Remaster is a top-down tank action game where armour angles, penetration and damaged modules decide every fight. Keep your thick front towards the enemy, slip around to their weaker sides and put a shell into the engine at the back.

Features

  • Real armour: strong front, weaker sides, a vulnerable rear with the engine. Shells bounce, stick or punch through.
  • Module damage: engine fires, broken tracks, a jammed turret ring, smashed vision blocks. Patch them in the field or reach the workshop.
  • Enemy tanks that spot you, radio your position to each other and search for you when you break contact.
  • Missions with primary and optional objectives and a respect score.
  • A sandbox to try every module combination.
